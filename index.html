Cross country race timing, run from your phones

Time the finish, record bib numbers and put together results with the phones your team already has. No timing mats, and no internet needed at the course.

How race day works

A coach runs the race. Helpers at the finish use their own phones.

1

Set up

Add your teams and runners, or import them from a spreadsheet or a previous race. Then create the race and send it to your helpers' phones.

2

Run the race

A timer taps once for every runner who finishes. A bib recorder says or types each bib number in finish order.

3

Get results

Collect the times and bibs, and the app puts them together into team and individual results, ready to share.

Made for the finish line

Built with coaches, for the way cross country races really go.

Works without internet

Phones pair by scanning a QR code and send races, times and bibs straight to each other.

Catches mistakes

A missed tap, an extra tap or a mixed-up bib is flagged, and the app walks you through fixing it before you save.

Say the bib numbers

Bib recorders can call out numbers instead of typing them. Speech is recognized on the phone.

Share results your way

Send results as a PDF, export them to Google Sheets, or send them to spectators' phones nearby.

Your rosters, ready to go

Import runners from a spreadsheet or Google Sheets, and reuse teams from earlier races.

Practice first

Try a practice race with your helpers before the real one, so everyone knows what to tap.
